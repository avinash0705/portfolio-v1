**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Active

## Relationship to Philosophy

This document is not the philosophy document, and should not be read as a restatement of it.

`000-philosophy.md` defines **how decisions get made** — the principles, values, and standards that every future decision must be checked against. It answers: *given a choice, which option wins, and why?*

This document defines **why the portfolio exists at all** — the problem it solves, who it is for, and what it needs to have achieved by a fixed point in time. It answers: *what are we trying to accomplish, and for whom?*

Philosophy is timeless and does not change based on outcomes. Vision is time-bound and should be revisited and re-evaluated — particularly the one-year success definition, which will eventually be judged as met, partially met, or missed.

If a proposed piece of work satisfies this Vision but violates Philosophy, Philosophy wins. If a proposed piece of work satisfies Philosophy but does not serve this Vision, it should not be built.

---

## Why This Portfolio Exists

The best engineering opportunities are earned through demonstrated capability rather than claimed expertise. This portfolio exists to make that capability visible.

Most engineering portfolios fail to do this. They list technologies instead of demonstrating judgment, and they ask to be trusted rather than allowing trust to be earned.

This portfolio exists to close that gap: to give any qualified evaluator direct, self-serve evidence of how its author identifies problems, makes trade-offs, and ships production software — without requiring a conversation to unlock that evidence.

---

## Audiences

### Primary Audience

**Technical decision-makers evaluating the author for senior engineering opportunities** — hiring managers, engineering leaders, and technical interviewers who need to assess whether this person can be trusted with ownership of important systems, and who are capable of judging engineering substance (not just presentation) when they see it.

This audience will spend limited time per visit, will look for signal over volume, and will trust specifics over summaries.

### Secondary Audience

**Engineering peers and the broader technical community** — other engineers who may encounter the portfolio through shared writing (journal entries, case studies) rather than through a hiring process. This audience is not evaluating the author for a role, but their engagement (references, shares, discussion) extends the portfolio's reach into the primary audience indirectly.

The portfolio is built for the primary audience first. Where the two audiences' needs diverge, the primary audience's needs take precedence.

## Long-Term Vision

The portfolio is not a static artifact produced once and left unchanged. It is intended to function as a **living engineering record** that grows in parallel with the author's career.

Over time, it should:

- Accumulate a growing body of case studies, each documenting a real problem, the decision made, and its outcome.
- Reflect current work rather than dated projects — content that no longer represents the author's present level of judgment should be retired or clearly marked as historical.
- Become a durable reference the author can point to across multiple job searches, engagements, and career stages, rather than something rebuilt from scratch each time.
- Establish enough independent credibility that it does part of the evaluation work before a conversation ever happens.

The portfolio's long-term measure of success is whether it continues to be an honest, current reflection of the author's engineering ability — not whether it stays visually or structurally unchanged.

---

## Success After One Year

One year after launch, this portfolio will be considered successful if:

1. **It has directly influenced at least one meaningful career outcome** — an interview, an offer, or a professional opportunity — where the evaluator references specific content from the portfolio (a case study, a decision, a metric) as a reason for their confidence.
2. **It contains a substantive, current body of evidence** — multiple real case studies and journal entries — rather than placeholder or aspirational content.
3. **It has required no rebuild.** Growth happens by adding to the existing structure, not by discarding and restarting it, indicating the original architecture and information design were sound.
4. **A first-time visitor from the primary audience can, unprompted, correctly describe what kind of engineer the author is** after a short visit — without needing anything explained to them in person first.

If, after one year, the portfolio has not produced any of the above, that is a signal to revisit this document — not a signal to silently keep extending the same approach.

## What This Document Is Not

This document makes no statement about frameworks, styling, hosting, folder structure, testing, or any other implementation or engineering decision. Those decisions belong to later documents in this series and must be made in service of the vision and success criteria stated here — not the reverse.
