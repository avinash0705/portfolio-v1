# Contact

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Active

---

## Relationship to Prior Documents

`003-information-architecture.md` places Contact as the terminal step in every persona's journey — "what's the next step?" per `000-philosophy.md`'s one-job table. `004-product-goals.md` explicitly excludes lead generation and sales funnels from this portfolio's scope. This document defines Contact as a page about **communication**, not conversion — its job is to help the right visitor start a conversation, and to help the wrong visitor realize, on their own, that this isn't the right channel for them.

---

## 1. Purpose

Contact answers **"what's the next step?"** for a visitor who has already formed an opinion elsewhere on the site (Homepage, a Case Study, Experience, Resume, or a Journal entry) and now wants to act on it.

Its job is narrow: make it obvious how to reach the author, for what kinds of conversations, and what to expect afterward. It is not a form designed to capture and convert every visitor — per `004-product-goals.md`'s Out of Scope, this page does not exist to generate leads.

---

## 2. Who Should Contact

The page should welcome, by name or clear description, the kinds of visitors this portfolio is actually built for (`002-user-personas.md`):

- Hiring managers and engineering leaders evaluating the author for a role.
- Technical recruiters with a genuinely relevant opportunity.
- Engineers who want to discuss something specific from a Case Study or Journal entry.
- Potential collaborators interested in AgentPrep.

Naming these groups explicitly does more work than any disclaimer could — a visitor who fits one of these descriptions immediately recognizes themselves, and a visitor who doesn't recognizes that too.

---

## 3. Who Should Not

The page should politely help the following senders self-select out, without being unwelcoming to anyone else:

- Generic sales outreach unrelated to engineering, product, or the author's work.
- Link-building or guest-post requests.
- Mass marketing or templated cold outreach.
- Recruitment agencies forwarding roles unrelated to the author's actual level or domain (per `002-user-personas.md`'s Recruiter persona, which assumes relevance, not volume).

The goal is not to sound guarded or unwelcoming. It's achieved the same way Section 2 works — by being specific about who the page is for and what conversations are welcome, so a mismatched sender can tell on their own that this isn't the right fit, without needing to be told "no" directly.

---

## 4. Contact Methods

Three primary methods, and no others by default:

1. **Email** — the universal, lowest-friction method, appropriate for every audience named in Section 2.
2. **LinkedIn** — appropriate for hiring managers, engineering leaders, and recruiters operating in a professional context.
3. **GitHub** — appropriate for engineers and collaborators who want to engage in a technical context.

No contact form is included by default. Email already serves that function without adding a maintained piece of the site, consistent with `000-philosophy.md`'s instruction to never introduce unnecessary dependencies. No phone number, scheduling link, or chat widget is included — none of the audiences in Section 2 require synchronous contact as a first step. Any additional method requires a clear, specific reason revisiting this document, not general convenience.

---

## 5. Response Expectations

Stated plainly, on the page itself, so no visitor is left guessing:

- Not every message receives a reply — particularly generic or mismatched outreach described in Section 3.
- Messages related to roles, technical leadership conversations, or discussion of published work are read personally and prioritised.
- Technical disagreement, questions, or discussion about a Case Study or Journal entry are genuinely welcome, not merely tolerated.
- Response times vary with availability; this is not a guaranteed-response or support channel.

This transparency is itself consistent with `000-philosophy.md`'s "Evidence Over Claims" — an honest expectation is more credible than an implied promise of prompt, universal responsiveness that may not hold.

---

## 6. Writing Style

The page reads as an invitation to a professional conversation, not a sales page or a plea for opportunities.

Avoid:
- Exaggerated enthusiasm ("Let's create something amazing together!").
- Urgency or scarcity framing ("Don't miss this opportunity to work with me").
- Generic inspirational language, per `000-philosophy.md`'s Communication Style.

Prefer something closer to:

> "If something you've read here resonates with your work, or you're interested in discussing engineering, product development, or opportunities, I'd be glad to hear from you."

Calm, first person, specific, and consistent with the identity established across every other page — the same voice, not a different one adopted just for this page.

---

## 7. Review Checklist

- [ ] The audiences in Section 2 are named clearly enough that a matching visitor recognizes themselves immediately.
- [ ] Section 3's audiences are addressed implicitly, through specificity, not through a defensive or unwelcoming tone.
- [ ] Only the three methods in Section 4 are present, with no unexplained additions.
- [ ] Response expectations (Section 5) are stated directly on the page, not left implied.
- [ ] No sentence uses exaggerated enthusiasm, urgency, or generic inspirational language.
- [ ] The page's voice is indistinguishable in tone from the rest of the site.

---

## What This Document Is Not

This document does not specify visual layout, component structure, or form implementation. It defines what the Contact page must communicate, to whom, and in what tone — implementation must satisfy this specification, not the reverse.
